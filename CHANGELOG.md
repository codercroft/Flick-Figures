## 2026-11-11: Nuke fix, Juicer direction, Firecracker out, Land Miner in
- Blastoid Nuke tier 3 (Quake Warheads): root cause of "the earthquake replaces the big hit": the Nuke path (tiers 1-3 stack) shrinks the rocket radius to 68 px, smaller than the rocket's distance to the figure it touches (74 px), so its blast never reached the target. A rocket that hits an opponent now (1) slams THAT opponent along the rocket's direction with `direct_hit_multiplier` (2.5) x the rocket's normal knockback on a touching figure (`blast_force` x the explosion multiplier x `direct_hit_falloff` 0.55, @exports on the ability; about 6000 px/s at tier 3, which flings a Grunt off the table), (2) the rocket's normal reduced-radius blast still goes off, and (3) every OTHER opponent gets the Earthquake shove (the target is excluded). `on_projectile_hit` now also receives the rocket's direction.
- Juicer: trail pieces push moving opponents the way Juicer was MOVING when it laid the piece again (it had been flipped to "back the way it came"); the on-hit splash still points outward and Slick tier 3 (steer to edge) is untouched. Description updated.
- Firecracker removed entirely: figure, ability, upgrade tree, icon (the FIRECRACKER icon slot became MINE), registry/draft/shop entries, the team explosion immunity (`team_has_firecracker`, `blast_shield` ring, `grants_explosion_immunity`, Safety Officer cover, lingering cover), Heavy Charges, the AI's firecracker bonuses and `_chain_blast_value`. Explosions set off by the player's side still never push player figures; ENEMY explosions now push everyone, their own side included (`Figure.blast_immune(victim, source_team)`). The set-off system (`detonate`, `detonate_allies`, `sets_off_team_explosives`, the registry explosive tag) stays for Bombo's Shrapnel and Blastoid's set-off rocket (Blastoid's text no longer mentions Firecracker). Saved runs: a Firecracker in the deck (or picks) loads as a Land Miner with its upgrades dropped (`RunSave.REMOVED_FIGURES`); run history keeps old names (nothing to migrate).
- Encounters: Tinderbox = Bombo + 2 Land Miners; Fireworks = 2 Land Miners + 2 Speedos + Magnet; Powder Keg = Land Miner + Aggro-bom + 2 Bombos.
- New Uncommon figure Land Miner (see STATE.md): average stats, drops a mine every 700 px of its own flick travel (a full-power flick travels ~1590 px so 2 mines; the first after 700 px), max 8 live per Land Miner, mines are non-blocking team-colored markers that explode (radius 140, force 600) when an opposing figure's center comes within 70 px; friendly-explosion rules, Blast Cap and Glow-in-the-dark apply; explosions never set mines off. Tree Multi-Mine (spacing x0.67 -> about 3 mines; force +40%; spacing x0.746 more so x0.5 overall -> about 4 mines, because multipliers compound) and Smart Bombs (+30% radius/force; spacing x0.8 and +15% more; tier 3 homing mines, range 250, drift up to 220 px/s toward moving opponents). AI: avoids player mines on its paths, values shoving player figures across its own mines, and Land Miner flicks (including a dedicated mine-laying reposition candidate) are scored by the player flick lanes their mines would cover.
- Fixed on the way: a typed lambda in the mine cap filter crashed on freed mines (the ability keeps an untyped array).
- Tests: new `tools/land_miner_test.gd` (registry/encounters/save conversion, drop spacing and cap, trigger and friendly-blast rules, homing, upgrade numbers, enemy AI turns, Nuke direct hit and earthquake, Juicer direction).
- Open: the test fakes nothing about mine-laying AI quality (it only checks that enemy Land Miner turns complete); homing/blast numbers are first guesses.

## 2026-11-09: Sandbox mode
- Main menu "Sandbox" opens the REAL combat scene (`main.tscn`) driven by `SandboxSetup` (static setup data: arena, act/Ascension, lineup kind boss/encounter/none, placed figure entries, player and enemy relics, player powers, AI off, free flick). `SandboxSetup.prepare()` fills RunManager's in-memory combat fields and the scene is built as normal; structural changes (arena, boss, encounter, act, Ascension, relics/powers via Apply, Reset, Clear) rewrite the setup and reload the scene. It never saves, unlocks, records history or marks the Compendium (nothing calls `autosave`, `mark_seen` or `end_run`; `_check_game_over` returns false so it never ends; `tools/sandbox_test.gd` checks the files are untouched).
- `SandboxPanel` (`scripts/sandbox_panel.gd`, CanvasLayer 25): a strip above the bottom bar (always visible; Setup/Hide, Edit/Play, AI on/off, Free on/off, Menu, Reset, Clear, Pause/Resume, Step) and a collapsible setup sheet with tabs Arena (every layout incl. Fortress Walls, Boss field, "Auto"), Figures (side Player/Enemy, every draftable figure plus Grunt, Swarmling, Sniper, Mini, Skull Ball, Clustling, slimes, Wall Piece; picking one folds the sheet away and Edit mode lets you tap the board or the deck strip to place it), Boss (any of the 6 with Act 1-3 and Ascension 0-10 selectors, so A8 boss upgrades run through the real `_apply_boss_ascension`), Fights (every regular and elite encounter in one tap) and Items (player or enemy relics, player powers, Apply reloads).
- Edit mode: tap a figure to select (ring), drag to move (player figures inside the deck strip are on deck), tap empty board to place; the Figures tab shows the selected figure with Delete and Upgrade (path + tier), Finish and Equipment dropdowns (placed figures are rebuilt in place).
- Play controls: normal play against the AI; AI off = after your flick it is your turn to flick ONE enemy figure, then back to the player (`Main._manual_enemy`); Free flick = no turn order or flick cycle, any figure of either side flickable any time, flicks overlap (`Main._flick_pool`); Pause freezes the physics, Step runs 0.1 s.
- Small real-code changes: `RunManager.sandbox_encounter` (forces an encounter in `_build_lineup`), `RunManager.clear_lineup()`, `TopBar` hides Map/Inventory/money in the sandbox, the bonus-flick Skip button sits above the strip.
- Deviations: powers can only be granted to the player (the enemy has no powers); figures that come from a loaded lineup (boss or encounter) can be moved or deleted live but their gear cannot be edited and they come back on Reset (only placed figures are saved in the setup); relics and powers are applied with an Apply button (reload) rather than live; the setup sheet covers the lower part of the board while open (fold it away to see everything).
- New test: `tools/sandbox_test.gd` (placement, gear respawn, move, delete, reload, all 6 bosses at A0 and A8, all 25 encounters, all 11 arenas, AI off, free flick, isolation).
